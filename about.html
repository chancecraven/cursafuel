About Cursa Fuel Co.

Built by operators who understand what it costs when something does not show up.

Cursa exists because fuel is the lifeblood of modern operations, and too many organizations are forced to worry about whether it will be there when they need it most.

Why We Exist

We remove uncertainty from the fuel equation.

Most fuel companies sell gallons. They deliver when you call. They bill what they promised. That's the transaction. When you run low, you manage it. When you run out, that's your problem.

Cursa was built on a different premise. Fuel is not a commodity to us. It is the thing that keeps your generators running, your fleets moving, your facilities operating, and your communities functioning. When it fails, everything downstream fails with it.

Our mission is not simply to deliver fuel. Our mission is to deliver certainty when failure is not an option.

The Name

Cursa

Latin root meaning to run, to flow, to course forward without stopping.

That is not an accident. That is the whole point. Your job site does not stop. Your asphalt crew does not stop. Your fleet does not stop. And your fuel supply does not either.

Always Running. Always There.
