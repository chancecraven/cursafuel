Who We Serve

The organizations that cannot afford to stop.

Cursa exists for the people and facilities where failure is not an option. Hospitals. Power grids. Emergency agencies. Construction sites. If your operation depends on continuous power and reliable fuel, we're built for you.

Industries

Every sector. Same standard.

///

Hospitals & Healthcare Systems

Healthcare cannot operate without power. Cursa provides emergency generator fueling and scheduled delivery to hospitals, surgery centers, and healthcare campuses. When the grid fails, the fuel is already there.

///

Data Centers

Downtime is not a metric — it's a liability. Data centers rely on backup generators running at full capacity for extended periods. Cursa monitors tank levels remotely and delivers before your generator ever runs dry.

///

Emergency Management Agencies

When a storm or disaster hits, emergency management operations are the backbone of response. Cursa has experience supplying fuel to state and local agencies during active disaster events, when logistics chains break down everywhere else.

///

Fire & Police Departments

Public safety fleets run 24 hours a day. Cursa provides scheduled fleet fueling and emergency generator fueling to fire departments, police departments, and public safety facilities so responders are never standing at a pump.

///

Municipal Governments

From water treatment plants to public works yards, municipal governments manage fuel across multiple facilities and fleets. Cursa simplifies that with scheduled delivery, tank monitoring, and logistics coordination.

///

Utility Companies

Utility operations run equipment in the field and generators in the substations. Cursa delivers to staging areas, work sites, and fixed facilities — supporting both routine operations and storm restoration response.

///

Telecommunications Providers

Cell towers and communications infrastructure run on backup generators. When those generators need fuel during a prolonged outage, Cursa has the logistics and reach to keep them running across multiple sites simultaneously.

///

Construction & General Contractors

Job sites run on diesel. Cursa delivers off-road fuel directly to your site, offers portable trailer rentals, and provides wet hosing for large equipment. Your crew starts moving, not waiting on a fuel truck.

///

Manufacturing & Distribution

Production facilities and distribution centers depend on generators, forklifts, and fleet vehicles. Cursa supplies bulk diesel, gasoline, and DEF on a schedule that matches your operation — not ours.

///

Schools & Universities

Campus generators protect students, staff, and critical systems during outages. Cursa manages fuel for educational institutions with the same reliability it delivers to hospitals and data centers.

///

Event Organizers

Festivals, outdoor events, and temporary installations need fuel that shows up on time. Cursa provides event fueling with portable trailer rental and on-site logistics support.

///

Disaster Recovery Contractors

When disaster recovery contractors mobilize, they need a fuel partner who moves with them. Cursa supports large-scale recovery operations with rapid mobilization, multi-site delivery, and consistent supply across extended events.
