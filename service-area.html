Where We Operate

Six-state primary coverage. National emergency reach.

Cursa maintains primary service operations across the Southeast with the logistics network and emergency partnerships to deploy nationally when disaster demands it.

Primary Service Area

Southeast Operations

These six states represent our core delivery footprint — where Cursa trucks run daily, tank monitoring is active, and emergency response is fastest.

SC

South Carolina

Full-service delivery statewide. Emergency generator fueling, fleet fueling, bulk delivery, and tank monitoring available across all major metro and rural areas.

NC

North Carolina

Scheduled and on-demand delivery throughout North Carolina. Disaster response capability active given the state's hurricane and flood exposure.

GA

Georgia

Coverage across Georgia including metro Atlanta and the coastal corridor. Critical infrastructure fueling and fleet operations supported statewide.

FL

Florida

Florida's hurricane exposure makes reliable fuel delivery essential. Cursa provides pre-storm staging, emergency fueling, and recovery support across the state.

TN

Tennessee

Full delivery operations in Tennessee. Generator fueling for municipal and healthcare facilities, fleet fueling, and bulk delivery for construction and logistics.

AL

Alabama

Statewide coverage in Alabama including industrial and coastal operations. Emergency response capability aligned with Gulf Coast weather patterns.

National Emergency Deployment

When a large-scale disaster demands it, Cursa mobilizes beyond its primary service area. Through emergency response partnerships and logistics networks, we can deploy nationally to support recovery operations. No event has been too large.
